# Playtest script: v0.40.0

About 20 minutes. Use a **test build** (`./build-zip.sh`), so skills work from level 1, cooldowns are
short, factions turn on with one member, and buffs last 20 seconds. Keep `godot.log` open afterwards
(`%APPDATA%\Brotato\logs\godot.log`).

## 1. It loads
1. Start Brotato with mods on. The mod list shows **FixedCoopSynergies** by Redsuperninja, version 0.40.0.
2. In the log: `[Synergies] loaded Fixed Coop Synergies v0.40.0` with no `SCRIPT ERROR` above it.

## 2. Stats never go negative (the main fix)
1. Start a run as **Knight** (Shield Wall: +Armor, +Dodge). Note Armor and Dodge in the pause screen.
2. Press **Space** in a wave, pause: Armor and Dodge are higher. Wait 20 seconds, pause again: both are
   back to what you noted, never below it.
3. Repeat with **Ghost** (Phase: big Dodge) and **Speedy** (Sprint: Speed). Same result.
4. Let the wave end while a buff is active. In the shop, every stat is back to normal.

## 3. Damage and XP buffs now work
1. Play **Wounded** (Adrenaline: +Damage). Cast, pause: **Damage** is higher, and weapon damage numbers
   go up on screen.
2. Play **Pacifist** (Harmony): XP Gain goes up while it lasts; Harvesting stays up until the wave ends.

## 4. Factions (co-op, 2 players)
1. Pick **Brawler** and **Dwarf** (Iron Fists). At wave start both players gain Melee Damage and Max HP;
   Dodge drops by 3 but never below 0.
2. Pick two **Hustlers** (e.g. Saver and Jack): Damage drops by 5 at most and never goes negative for a
   player who had 0; Harvesting rises.
3. On the character-select screen the legend shows 13 factions, and every character has a coloured border.

## 5. Enemies recover
1. Play **Loud** (Sonic Boom freezes everything) next to **Doctor** (Field Hospital slows). Overlap the
   freeze and the slow zone. When both end, enemies move at normal speed and lose the blue tint.
2. Play **Romantic** (Charm): charmed enemies go back to normal speed afterwards.

## 6. Materials
1. Play **Saver** (Cash Out): your materials go up by 15 on each cast (test build, tier 1).

## Report
Note anything that differs, with the character, the step, and the matching lines from `godot.log`.
